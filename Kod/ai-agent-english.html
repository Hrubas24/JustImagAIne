AI Agent

AI Agent that acts on your behalf

Software intelligence capable of planning, evaluating, and executing complex tasks in several steps without human control. It converts repetitive and variable processes into autonomous logic.

I want an AI Agent Where to use it

Capabilities

What your AI Agent can do

01

Independent work 24/7

The agent works continuously without human constraints. It doesn't get tired, doesn't forget, and doesn't lose focus — completing tasks accurately and reliably even in the middle of the night.

02

Autonomous decision-making

It evaluates situations, weighs options, and decides independently — according to rules you set. It doesn't need your approval for every step.

03

Automated actions

It handles tasks that would otherwise require manual intervention — sending messages, updating databases, filling out forms, triggering processes.

04

System integration

It connects internal and external systems and lets them work together. CRM, ERP, email platforms, custom APIs — the agent acts as an orchestrator for the whole stack.

05

Evaluates data

It analyzes information in real time, identifies patterns and anomalies, and presents conclusions — without the need for manual spreadsheet or dashboard analysis.

06

Minimizes errors

It eliminates human errors caused by fatigue, inattention, or overload. The agent always follows the configured process — consistently and precisely.

Use cases

Where an AI Agent works best

Internal company processes

Automating administration, managing workflows, and processing internal requests without human oversight.

  • Managing internal tickets and requests
  • Automatic report generation
  • Notifications and case escalation

Document processing

Reading, analyzing, classifying, and extracting data from documents — invoices, contracts, forms, emails.

  • Data extraction from invoices and contracts
  • Document classification and routing
  • Automatic database population

Monitoring & alerting

Continuous oversight of systems, data, or the market — with automatic alerts for exceptions.

  • Tracking KPIs and anomalies
  • Automatic alerts to Slack, email
  • Competitor and market monitoring

How it works

From task to result without your intervention

  1. 01

    Setting the goal

    We define what the agent should do and what result it should deliver.

  2. 02

    Planning

    The agent breaks the task into steps and determines the order of actions.

  3. 03

    Execution

    It performs actions, calls APIs, processes data, and makes decisions.

  4. 04

    Evaluation

    It checks the results and corrects deviations from the goal.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    The result is ready — you only get what matters.
