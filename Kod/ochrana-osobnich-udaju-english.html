2. Our Approach to Processing Personal Data

We process only the personal data necessary to provide our services. Processing is carried out transparently, securely, and in accordance with GDPR.

As part of our activities, we use artificial intelligence (AI language models), primarily for the operation of AI chatbots that we develop for our Clients. We do not use AI for automated individual decision-making or profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR. The outputs of AI systems are subject to the possibility of human review, and our Clients have the option to configure or limit the extent of automation.

3. In What Capacity We Act With Regard to Personal Data

This is key to our activities — depending on the context, we are either a Controller or a Processor.

3.1 Just ImagAIne as a Controller

With respect to you (a website visitor, prospective or existing Client), we are the Controller of personal data. We process data you provide to us yourself (name, email, billing details, etc.) — see Art. 4 and 5 for an overview.

3.2 Just ImagAIne as a Processor

If we develop and operate an AI chatbot for a Client, End Users' communication with that chatbot constitutes processing of personal data that we carry out on behalf of and under the instructions of the Client — i.e., in the role of Processor. In this relationship, the Client is the Controller and is responsible, among other things, for informing its End Users about the processing.

The terms of this processing (scope, purpose, duration, security) are governed by a separate Data Processing Agreement (DPA), which we conclude with the Client as part of our cooperation.

If you are an End User of a chatbot deployed by one of our Clients and wish to exercise your rights regarding personal data, please contact the relevant Client directly — this policy does not apply to processing carried out on its behalf.

3.3 Sub-processors

In providing services (both as Processor and as Controller), we use other entities (see Art. 7), in particular providers of AI language models, the chatbot operating platform, and Airtable, where the End User's IP address is stored for rate-limiting and chatbot abuse-protection purposes. We have appropriate agreements in place with these entities (DPA / data processing terms).

4. What Personal Data We Process About You

  • Name and surname, or business ID if you are a sole trader/company
  • Contact details (email, phone)
  • Billing and bank details (necessary for invoicing and accounting)
  • The content of our mutual communication (inquiries, questions, consultations)
  • Operational and technical data related to the delivered service (e.g., access credentials to the chatbot environment)
  • IP address and basic technical data about your website visit (necessary cookies for site functionality)
  • The IP address of an AI chatbot's End User — we store it to protect the service against abuse and to manage query limits (rate limiting); we process this data on behalf of the Client as a Processor (see Art. 3.2 and Art. 5)

We do not process any special categories of personal data (sensitive data) about you.

(We currently do not operate a newsletter or analytics/marketing cookies. If we introduce these in the future, we will update this section and the related consents.)

6. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data on the basis of:

  • performance of a contract — so that we can provide you with our services,
  • compliance with a legal obligation — e.g., bookkeeping,
  • legitimate interest — e.g., handling inquiries, basic website operation, service security,
  • consent — if we request it from you in the future (e.g., when introducing a newsletter).

If we process your personal data as a Processor on behalf of a Client, the legal basis follows what the Client, as Controller, has chosen with respect to its End Users.

8. Security of Personal Data

Technical measures:

  • encryption of data transmission (SSL/TLS), secure HTTPS protocol
  • regular data backups
  • limited and protected access to systems and access credentials
  • development with regard to the principle of privacy by design

Organizational measures:

  • personal data is processed only by Matyáš Hrubý, or contractually bound subcontractors bound by confidentiality
  • access to systems is protected by individual passwords

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • access the personal data we process about you,
  • rectification of inaccurate data,
  • erasure of data once the reason for its processing has ceased,
  • restriction of processing,
  • portability of the data you provided to us,
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest,
  • withdraw consent, if processing is based on consent,
  • not be subject to automated individual decision-making — we do not carry this out.

You can exercise your rights at mhruby@justimagaine.com. We will respond no later than within one month.

If you are not satisfied with how your request was handled, you have the right to file a complaint with the Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ), Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Prague 7, www.uoou.cz.
